We have a predicting whether a patient has a medical condition or not. Two models give estimates of 48% and 51% probability, with a cutoff for classification being 50% . Even though these estimates are very cloes, they have different classifications with heavy implications. What should we do in these cases, and is it reasonable for a small change like in this case to result in two different decisions?
