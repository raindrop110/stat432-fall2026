Imagine you are trying to predict whether a customer will purchase a product based on two predictors: age and annual income. The probability of purchasing the product changes in a complex, nonlinear way across these two variables, and customers with similar ages and incomes tend to make similar purchasing decisions. Would you choose logistic regression or KNN and why? Explain. Are there any situations in which the model you select might perform worse than the other?
